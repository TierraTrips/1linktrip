1. Open Advanced DNS

  1. Namecheap → Domain List → Manage next to 1linktrip.com → Advanced DNS tab.

3. One mail record (MX)

Check first. Scroll down to Mail Settings. Right now it says Email Forwarding (that's what forwards any @1linktrip.com addresses you've set up). Adding this record means switching it to Custom MX, which can stop those forwards. Look at Domain tab → Redirect Email: if you have forwards there you want to keep, stop here and tell Hayden. If it's empty or you don't use them, carry on.
  1. Under Mail Settings, pick Custom MX.
